We may add website analytics or advertising (such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense) in the future. If we do, we will update this page and explain how cookies are used.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies at this time. Third-party services mentioned above may set cookies in your browser according to their own policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our books are for children, but this website is meant for parents, teachers and business owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
