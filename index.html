Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to common questions.

What is included in the Starter plan (₹1,000 / month)?

Google Business Profile setup, 12 social media posts every month, and WhatsApp Business setup.

What is included in the Starter + Ads plan (₹2,500 / month)?

Everything in the Starter plan, plus management of your Instagram, Facebook (Meta) and Google Ads campaigns.

Does the monthly fee include the ad budget?

No. The monthly fee is for our service only. The ad budget is paid by the shop directly to Meta or Google, and your ad accounts always stay in your own name.

Do you guarantee the first place on Google?

No. Nobody can guarantee a Google ranking. We set up everything correctly so that your business is easier to find.

Which areas do you serve?

Shops and small businesses across the whole of Kanyakumari district and Tirunelveli district, including Nagercoil and Radhapuram.

How long does a website take?

Basic 3-4 days, Standard 5-7 days and Premium 7-10 days, counted from the day we receive all your photos and details. Connecting your domain can take 1-2 extra days.

Will the website be mine?

Yes. The domain is bought in your name and you pay for it directly (₹800-1,500 a year). We only design and update the website.

Who will maintain the website?

We will. With the optional maintenance (₹500/month) we make 2 small changes every month, such as prices, photos or offers. Without maintenance, each change is ₹300.

How do I get started?

Send us a message on WhatsApp and tell us about your shop. We take care of the rest.
