Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Books on Amazon

Our books are sold and delivered by Amazon. For any order, return or refund on a book, please contact Amazon Customer Service. Amazon's own policy applies.

Digital products (PDF downloads)

If we sell digital products such as PDF books, workbooks or printables on this website, they are delivered online right after payment and cannot be returned. Because of this, we do not give refunds once the file has been delivered or downloaded.

We will gladly help or refund you in these cases:

  • You were charged but did not receive the file.
  • The file is damaged or will not open, and we cannot fix it.
  • You were charged twice for the same product.

Please write to us within 7 days of your purchase with your payment details, and we will reply within 2 working days.

Digital marketing services

Services are billed month by month. You can cancel at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or email before the next month starts. You pay only for the months already started.

Fees for work already done, such as designs, posts or account setup, are not refundable. If we have not started the work for a month you paid for, we will refund that month's fee in full.

The advertising budget is paid by you directly to Meta, Google or other platforms. We cannot refund ad spend, because it is not paid to us.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent to the same account or UPI ID you paid from, usually within 7 working days.
